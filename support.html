About Flip

Flip is a daily prediction game where everyone plays the same sequence of coin flips.

Predict whether the next flip will be Heads or Tails. Get it right and keep going. Get it wrong and your run ends until tomorrow.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

Choose Heads or Tails before the next flip. If your prediction is correct, your streak continues. If you're wrong, your run ends for the day.

Does everyone get the same flips?

Yes. Every player receives the same daily sequence of flips, allowing everyone to compete on an equal playing field.

What happens if I guess incorrectly?

Your streak ends and you'll be locked out until the next daily challenge begins.

When does the challenge reset?

The daily challenge resets once per day (midnight PDT). Your next opportunity begins when the new challenge becomes available.

How does the leaderboard work?

The leaderboard ranks players by their longest consecutive streak of correct predictions.

Can I play multiple times in one day?

You can continue making predictions as long as your current streak is alive. Once you make an incorrect prediction, you'll need to wait for the next daily challenge.

Is Flip gambling?

No. Flip does not involve wagering money or other items of value. It is a free prediction game.
